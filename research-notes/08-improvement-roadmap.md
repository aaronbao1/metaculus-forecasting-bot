## 1. Top 10 Improvements (ranked by expected effect × evidence strength / cost)

### 1. Ship a calibration layer now: backtest-fitted Platt vs fixed-α extremization, head-to-head — **IMMEDIATE, $0 replay**
- **What:** The live bot ships identity Platt (`bot/calibration.py`, `MIN_FIT_N=100` own resolutions = months away) while documented LLM yes-bias and hedging-toward-0.5 go uncorrected. Two arms of the *same transform family* (AIA Appendix G.2 proves Platt-with-b=0 ≡ Karmarkar extremization): (a) Platt fitted on temporally-split backtest history; (b) pre-registered fixed α=√3≈1.73 (Neyman-Roughgarden), which dodges the repo's legitimate "fitted extremization overfits" objection in `bot/aggregation.py` — that objection applies to fitted, not fixed coefficients. Expect optimal α at 1.1–1.5, not 1.73, because the 9 members share one research report (the decorrelated-information precondition for full extremization is weak).
- **Evidence:** Metaculus calibration analysis (notebook 43356): binary ΔBrier **+0.0164 (p=5.2e-4)**, MC **+0.0054 (p=5.3e-5)** — only method significant on both. AIA Forecaster (https://arxiv.org/html/2511.07678v1): fixed d=√3 0.1140→0.1085; fitted Platt 0.1076; **OOD-fitted Platt still beats identity** (0.1140→0.1104). Platt > isotonic below n≈1000 (Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana, https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~alexn/papers/calibration.icml05.crc.rev3.pdf). Flywheel code exists: `backtest/experiments/export_calibration.py` + `allow_calibration=True` (`docs/backtesting/COMPATIBILITY.md`).
- **Retrocast experiment:** Sort clean binary stratum by `scheduled_close_time`; fit Platt on first 60% (≥100 resolved) on `aggregate_raw` (pre-cap); replay variant `{calibration_dir: calibration-backtest}` on held-out 40%; parallel arms α ∈ {1.0, 1.2, 1.41, 1.73, 2.0} pre-registered. Also 5-fold CV on run-1 via `artifacts_to_calibration_rows`. Metric: paired Brier + log score + reliability diagram, bootstrap CI (`backtest compare`); report fitted (a,b) — b≪0 directly measures yes-bias; log fraction of forecasts hitting caps per α. n: full stratum (~1k+ binary); needs ~500–1000 for 0.005-class effects. Free riders: beta calibration (3-param, Kull et al., https://proceedings.mlr.press/v54/kull17a.html) and BBC (https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27668, Beta-variance as uncertainty signal) in the same CV harness. Decision rule: ship `calibration/binary.json` if CV improvement's CI excludes 0; fixed-α is the fallback.
- **Cost:** **$0 replay.** Expected effect: +0.005–0.016 Brier — largest single documented lever.

### 2. Swap regressed ensemble members — newest ≠ best forecaster in all three families
- **What:** `bot/config.py` runs gpt-5.4 / claude-opus-4-6 / gemini-3.1-pro-preview. Two of three are measurably *worse* forecasters than their in-family predecessors; Sonnet-4.5 is also ~5x cheaper than Opus-4.6.
- **Evidence:** ForecastBench difficulty-adjusted baseline leaderboard (https://github.com/forecastingresearch/forecastbench-datasets, pulled 2026-06-11, N=2,542): Claude-Opus-4-6 60.4 [59.0,61.7] < Sonnet-4.5 62.8 [62.0,63.5] (**non-overlapping CIs**); Gemini-3.1-Pro-Preview 59.7 < Gemini-3-Pro 61.8; GPT-5.2 60.8 < GPT-5 62.0. **Conflict to note:** Prediction Arena (https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07355v1) found gemini-3.1-pro-preview its best paper-trader — trading P&L is a weaker proxy than difficulty-adjusted Brier at n=2.5k, but run the swap, don't assume.
- **Retrocast experiment:** Three independent one-member swaps, research stage frozen (cached): {opus-4-6→sonnet-4.5}, {gemini-3.1→gemini-3-pro}, {gpt-5.4→gpt-5}. Metric: member-level paired Brier (one-member effects dilute ~3x at ensemble level — report both). n=300/swap.
- **Cost:** **Cheap LLM** (~$25–40/swap; member calls only). Expected: ~1–2 Brier-Index points + cost reduction. Highest-value cheap experiment on the board.

### 3. Buy real diversity: correlation matrix ($0, today) → Grok / decorrelated 4th member
- **What:** 9 members = 3 families × 3 runs, identical prompt, identical shared research, temp 0.5 — effective n is far below 9. Step 1 measures it free; step 2 reallocates the call budget to a decorrelated family.
- **Evidence:** Replicated twice independently: ICML 2026 WS "Diversity is the strength of the AI crowd" (https://openreview.net/forum?id=JAYM7AGe50 — Grok's errors least correlated with Gemini-3-Pro/GPT-5; extra correlated samples add ~nothing) and Mantic × Thinking Machines (https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/training-llms-to-predict-world-events/ — "Grok 4 is the least replaceable" in their optimal 40/20/20/20 ensemble). Mechanism: Correlated Errors in LLMs (Kim et al., ICML 2025, https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.07962) — larger/more accurate models err *together* (60% agreement conditional on both erring, across providers). Grok 4.20 is now the strongest single-model forecaster on ForecastBench tournament (68.1, p(supers>it)=0.03). Note: Grok is NOT covered by Metaculus's free OpenRouter credits.
- **Retrocast experiment:** **Step 1 ($0, IMMEDIATE):** script over run-1 `artifacts.jsonl` — member×member error-correlation matrix in log-odds, intra- vs inter-family, effective-n. **Step 2 (cheap):** fixed 9-call budget, variant runs=2/family + `x-ai/grok-4.20` ×2 + 1 open decorrelated model; metric trimmed-GMO paired Brier; n=400. **Gate everything downstream on step 1:** if inter-family correlation >~0.8, decorrelated members beat any operator tweak; if intra ≈ inter, skip split-view/persona-style work entirely.
- **Cost:** $0 then cheap LLM (~$50). Expected: free accuracy from reallocation; trimmed-GMO can only monetize disagreement that exists.

### 4. Agentic gap-fill research (one-shot → multi-query + second pass) — the #1 winner theme, our largest architecture gap
- **What:** `bot/research.py` makes a single AskNews call **with the raw question title as the query** (no query generation — vs Halawi's ~12 generated queries), no relevance filter, no re-query. All 9 members anchor on the same one-shot dossier — the correlated-miss failure mode.
- **Evidence:** #1 AI-summarized theme of Fall-2025 winner advice (Metaculus notebook 43357); Berman "One Shot Is Not Enough" (thonks.substack.com): one-shot→ReAct dropped divergence-trigger rate 21%→6% over ~600 q; AIA ablation: agentic > non-agentic search (~0.009 Brier); nostreambot (9th Fall) gap-fill second pass; distinct-source count was the #1 winner predictor (r=0.42); 43363 grades it "Strong". Largest research-side effects in literature (retrieval ≈0.02 Brier, Halawi).
- **Retrocast experiment:** Staged. (a) `_asknews_multiquery_research` in `backtest/timetravel/providers.py` (provider contract is runtime setattr — zero `bot/` edits): cheap LLM generates 3–5 queries (include resolution-criteria thresholds, not just title), fan out AskNews-historical, dedupe, relevance-filter. Variant `{research_providers: [asknews_multiquery, markets_historical]}`. (b) Second arm: gap-driven round 2 (`research_rounds: 2` in `BotConfig` — parser_model extracts ≤3 missing facts → targeted re-query). Research cache invalidated → new fan-out: n=200 screening ≈ $50–75/arm; escalate to 500 if directional. Metric: paired spot/baseline score + evidence-ledger doc count as mediator + Murphy *resolution* component (hypothesis predicts resolution gains, not calibration gains).
- **Cost:** **Cheap LLM** (~$50–75/arm). Expected: 2–5 peer-pts/q — plausibly the biggest single live delta.

### 5. Numeric distribution machinery (pooling, tails, log-scale bypass, OOB mass) — largest per-type headroom
- **What:** Numeric/discrete is bots' worst deficit class (−23.2 vs Pros head-to-head; MC −32.9 vs binary −14.8) and the scoring is asymmetric: pdf floored at 0.01 / capped at 35 (`backtest/scoring/metaculus_scores.py`) — a thin-tail miss costs ln(0.01)=−4.6 while a perfect spike earns +3.6. Four verified code gaps: (i) quantile-median Vincentization (`_median_per_percentile`, `bot/ensemble_bot.py`) is the *sharpness-maximizing* pool in a tail-punishing rule; `aggregate_cdfs` is dead code; (ii) no tail recalibration exists (Platt is binary-only); (iii) **log-scaled (zero_point) questions silently bypass the entire PCHIP stack** (`bot/ensemble_bot.py:213-216`); (iv) **open-bound tail mass hard-pinned to 0.001** (`bot/distributions.py:49-50`) — an OOB resolution scores ≈ −196 baseline points; model beliefs about out-of-bounds outcomes are discarded.
- **Evidence:** Lichtendahl/Grushka-Cockayne/Winkler 2013 (https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.1120.1667): quantile averaging always sharper, probability averaging wider and better when components overconfident — and LLMs are systematically overconfident (OpenEstimate https://arxiv.org/html/2510.15096v1; FermiEval https://arxiv.org/pdf/2510.26995 — 70–80% coverage at 90% nominal). Tournament practice: fattening tails was nostreambot's single biggest numeric gain (https://github.com/No-Stream/metaculus-bot, `tail_widening.py` recipe). Theory and practice point opposite directions on pooling — exactly what Retrocast resolves.
- **Retrocast experiment:** Prerequisite: extend `from_member_forecasts` past binary (or ~30-line replay script over `member_percentile_dicts` in stage records). Then ONE numeric corpus pass (qtypes [numeric, discrete], n≈150–250, ~$40–60) unlocks all-$0 replays: (a) pooling 3-arm: vincent-median (current) vs pointwise-mean of member PCHIP CDFs vs pointwise-median, plus +5% uniform-mixture floor arm; (b) tail-widen k ∈ {1.0, 1.15, 1.3, 1.5, 2.0} in nostream's transformed space + PIT histogram diagnosis, fit on date-split train, report held-out only; (c) log-scaled PCHIP grid fix (evaluate PCHIP at `scaled_location` grid points — n≈60 log-scaled, deterministic paired); (d) OOB-mass elicitation is a prompt change (new spend, ~$0.3/q): elicit P(below min)/P(above max), clamp [0.001, 0.15], pass into `percentiles_to_cdf`. Metric throughout: paired continuous baseline/log score + PIT coverage.
- **Cost:** ~$40–60 corpus run, then **$0 replays**. Expected: several points/q on the numeric slice — likely the largest per-question headroom anywhere.

### 6. MC machinery: per-option calibration, floor×pool grid, option-order shuffle — worst deficit, least machinery
- **What:** MC gets per-option arithmetic mean + 0.01 floor (`bot/aggregation.py`) — the same linear pooling that lost to GMO on binary — with NO calibration layer, unmitigated option-order bias (all 9 members see identical platform order, so position bias does not average out), and ordinal-bucket questions treated as unordered categories.
- **Evidence:** 43356: per-option logistic recalibration MC ΔBrier +0.0054 (p=5.3e-5); uniform shift actively *harms* MC. Zheng et al. ICLR 2024 (https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.03882): MC selection bias, mitigated label-free by permutation (ensemble-PriDe is free in call count — shuffle order per member, parse by name). Log-pooling analog: Sevilla GMO result (https://forum.effectivealtruism.org/posts/sMjcjnnpoAQCcedL2/when-pooling-forecasts-use-the-geometric-mean-of-odds).
- **Retrocast experiment:** Needs the MC corpus (see Structural bets). Then: **$0 replay grid** `mc_floor ∈ {0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03, 0.05}` × `mc_pool ∈ {mean, trimmed_gmo_renorm}` + per-option Platt in log-odds before renormalize + 2-option-MC→binary routing (one-line dispatch) + ordinal smoothing (post-hoc, $0). MC shuffle is cache-breaking (~$0.3/q/variant, n≈150; position-1-inflation diagnostic detectable at n≈40 without resolutions). Metric: paired MC baseline score + Brier.
- **Cost:** $0 replays once corpus exists; shuffle cheap LLM. Expected: floor sweep plausibly 3–8 pts/q on MC if floored-winner events occur at ≥3%; rest smaller but free.

### 7. Conditional divergence supervisor — resurrect the descoped seam, gated on spread
- **What:** Removed in commit 6d9c0f7; only `spread` provenance remains. Evidence has since hardened that *conditional* stacking is the active ingredient. Two fixes to the trigger first: compute spread in **log-odds, not probability space** (0.90 vs 0.98 is informationally huge, prob-spread 0.08), and add a bimodality statistic (largest internal gap in sorted log-odds) — bimodal disagreement (half the ensemble thinks the event already happened) is exactly the case one extra research call resolves, and trim already handles single outliers.
- **Evidence:** nostreambot CONDITIONAL_STACKING (median when agreeing; stacker rewrite when binary spread >0.15) is core to the top open-source bot, and his retrospective: **stacking on all questions increased variance with no gain** (github.com/No-Stream/metaculus-bot, nostream.substack.com). AIA supervisor ≈0.005 Brier; Berman's fires ~1-in-6. Counterpoint priced in: AIA's disagreement supervisor added only ~0.0015 over mean — cheap variance signals carry most of the information.
- **Retrocast experiment:** **Phase A ($0, IMMEDIATE):** bucket run-1 questions by log-odds spread quintile; report mean baseline score + h2h_market/h2h_cp per quintile, Spearman(spread, Brier), incremental AUC of bimodality on "confidently wrong". **Decision rule: build only if top-quintile h2h_market < −4 points** (else headroom can't repay spend). **Phase B (~$25):** supervisor prompt (member reasonings + research → reconciled prob, log-odds blend — reference implementation in git history, commits 2e54153/f2ed9f1) on cached high-spread questions only, full 1.5k stratum → ~250 triggered q. Test "reads reasonings" separately from "does follow-up research" — the first is far cheaper.
- **Cost:** $0 then ~$25. Expected: ~0.005 Brier concentrated on the hardest sixth.

### 8. Cap-width / asymmetric caps / n_trim grid — **IMMEDIATE, $0 replay**
- **What:** Caps [0.02, 0.98] are hardcoded, never measured; `n_trim` isn't a config field (`aggregate_binary` dispatches with no kwargs, so trimmed_gmo is locked at 1/side). Asymmetric caps are principled: base rates aren't 50% and yes-bias is one-sided.
- **Evidence:** Capping is the strongest within-winner correlate (r=+0.48, p=0.005, Fall survey 43337) — but bots lose on *discrimination* (Pros 44pp yes/no gap vs bots 26pp), and a justified 0.5% capped to 2% bleeds points in zero-sum peer scoring. The right setting depends on this bot's own confident-wrong rate — measurable in run-1.
- **Retrocast experiment:** Add `n_trim`/`extremize_a` to `BotConfig` (5-line change; perturbs config_hash — do once, deliberately). Replay grid (cap_lo, cap_hi) ∈ {0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.03} × {0.95, 0.97, 0.98, 0.99} + n_trim ∈ {0, 1, 2} over full clean stratum on `aggregate_raw`. Metric: mean baseline score + **count of capped-and-wrong events** + per-question quantiles (tail questions dominate). **Guard: never loosen caps on a green n=200 result** — diffs only exist on the ~20–40 cap-active questions, and the loss side is tail-shaped; loosen only at n≈1k+.
- **Cost:** **$0.** Expected: ±1–2 pts/q on cap-active questions; high confidence it's worth $0 to know.

### 9. Post-hoc market-price log-odds blend + de-double-counting — **IMMEDIATE, $0 artifact**
- **What:** Blend final' = σ((1−w)·logit(bot_raw) + w·logit(price_at_T)). Spot peer is strictly proper: reporting your true posterior *given* a public market price is truthful — distinct from the prohibited "optimize to match hidden CP". Follow-up: prices currently enter twice (members see quotes in research with documented 0.994-parroting risk, then any blend adds them again) — the clean design is price-free member research + one explicit post-hoc blend.
- **Evidence:** Harness already records `price_at_T`/`price_at_T_ts` per question (`backtest/experiments/runner.py:359-369`); Schoenegger et al. +17–28% from crowd info; AIA market anchoring; market-odds injection placed adjacentwire #11 alone. Live-path weakness compounds it: `bot/markets.py` keyword-searches with the full verbose title and silently drops the section on miss — the backtest's curated crosswalk **overstates live market-research quality**.
- **Retrocast experiment:** ($0) Join aggregation-stage `aggregate_raw` with `price_at_T`; sweep w ∈ {0…0.9}, 5-fold CV across qids; gate on price staleness (≤48h); paired bootstrap on covered subset; report coverage (may be <40% on Metaculus slice). Then 3 arms (~$50): A current (cached), B price-free research + blend, C both; diagnostics: member-spread shift and corr(member_mean, price). Plus free live-path diagnostic: run `market_research()` over the 200 titles, measure non-empty rate; test query distillation (strip to 3–6 keywords via parser_model) if low.
- **Cost:** **$0 artifact**, then cheap LLM. Expected: +2–6 baseline pts on covered questions.

### 10. Research composition: restore the outside-view track + Metaculus-siblings prior
- **What:** Run-1's spec patched providers to `[asknews_historical, markets_historical]`, dropping the search-LLM outside-view/base-rates track entirely — the baseline measures a bot that never sees a base-rate section, a live-vs-backtest divergence that quietly biases every comparison. Separately, no provider searches Metaculus itself for similar/resolved sibling questions.
- **Evidence:** Base rates/comparison classes carry the largest single human-side effect (Brier 0.17 vs 0.49 training decomposition, research doc); distinct-source count r=0.42 (#1 winner predictor). Siblings prior: 34% of winners vs 0% of non-winners (Fisher p=0.04, notebook 43337). **Caveat that shapes the design:** community-anchoring reverses *within* winners (r=−0.32) and prompt-optimizing toward community failed live (43362: +2.76 vs +4.71/q) — inject siblings as *evidence*, never as a target.
- **Retrocast experiment:** (a) Variant `{+outside: [asknews_historical, gdelt_wayback, exa_historical, markets_historical]}` on the same 200 questions (control fully cached), ~$50; metric: paired baseline score + Murphy resolution component; n=200, detectable ~2–3.5 pts. (b) New `metaculus_siblings` provider (API search + CP-as-of-cutoff + resolved siblings; leakage guard via `timetravel/asof.py`); n=300; slice by |bot − community| ≥15pts to test anchoring-drag directly.
- **Cost:** Cheap LLM (~$50 + ~$60). Expected: +2–8 pts/q if models don't already supply base rates parametrically; could be ~0 — either answer corrects the baseline's validity.

## 4. Rejected / Overhyped Ideas

| Idea | Verdict | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Persona-diversified ensembles** | Reject | Direct negative: 2,000+ personas, controlled ablation, no accuracy gain, forecasts "remarkably homogeneous" (https://arxiv.org/html/2511.02458). Decorrelate the *information set* instead. |
| **Prompt-wording optimization for frontier models** | Reject | RCT-grade null on o3 at n=278; optimized prompt failed live (+2.76 vs +4.71/q); gains only on weak models (notebook 43362). Cap at one negative-control variant. Structural stages only. |
| **Selecting variants by KL/divergence-to-community** | Hard rule, never | That's exactly how 43362's Pt-1 prompt failed live; winners *disagree* with the crowd (r=−0.32 within winners). Resolutions-only selection. |
| **Skill-weighted / fitted member weights** | Reject at current n | 50 years of forecast-combination-puzzle evidence (Smith & Wallis; Claeskens; Wang & Hyndman https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.04216); AIA's best-of-k was their *worst* aggregator (0.1191 vs 0.1140). Run the $0 LOO negative-result once; revisit only at n≥1000s with shrinkage. |
| **Hunting mean vs median vs GMO vs trimmed** | Stop testing | AIA Table 9 spread: 0.0004 Brier; ForecastBench 0.194 vs 0.197. n=200 detects ~0.012 — any "winner" is a coin flip. One full-stratum sanity replay, then freeze. |
| **Unconditional supervisor / stacking on all questions** | Reject | nostream measured it: variance up, no gain. Conditionality is the active ingredient. |
| **Full end-to-end agent refactor** | Reject | Native agent harnesses still bad at forecasting (FutureSim https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15188: best 25%, some negative skill); predictably's refactor = his two worst tournaments. Scaffolding lead persists. |
| **Prompt-simulated knowledge cutoffs** | Invalid methodology | 52% Brier gap (arXiv 2601.13717). Strict-cutoff backtests are the only ones that transferred live. |
| **Fitted extremization without holdout / isotonic below n≈1000** | Reject the form, not the family | Fixed pre-registered α or temporally-split Platt only; isotonic needs n≥1000 (ICML'05) and destroys smooth shrinkage the caps rely on. |
| **Loosening caps off small-n results** | Reject | Loss side is tail-shaped; one 0.001-vs-0.02 catastrophe is −896 vs −464 baseline points. Tighten/asymmetrize on data; loosen only at n≈1k+. |
| **Research-budget maximalism** | Caution | Prediction Arena: research volume uncorrelated with outcomes (https://arxiv.org/html/2604.07355v1). Spend on *query quality and decorrelation*, not volume. |
| **Hazard burn-down adjustments at tournament horizons** | Deprioritize | Spot scoring at close-7d mostly neutralizes it; `bot/timeutils.py` stays dead code unless 365d-horizon replays say otherwise. |
| **Martingale Score, hidden-state probes, resolution screenshot verifier** | Defer | Need step-wise prob extraction / white-box weights / live page state respectively — infra-gated, not evidence-gated. |
| **"Newest model = best forecaster"** | Empirically false | All three families regressed (ForecastBench baseline, non-overlapping CIs) — see Top-10 #2. |

## 5. Open Questions Run-1 Data Will Answer

Look for these in the baseline report; each carries a pre-registered decision rule:

1. **Effective ensemble n** (correlation matrix): inter-family log-odds error correlation >0.8 → prioritize Grok/open-weight member over all operator tweaks; intra ≈ inter → kill split-view and paraphrase variants. Subset curve: 3×2 ≈ 3×3 → cut to 6 members, fund research depth.
2. **Is the ensemble already calibrated?** Reliability term ≈ 0 in Murphy decomposition → ship identity, skip Top-10 #1's fitted arm; fitted b≪0 quantifies yes-bias (compare mean forecast vs realized base rate — research doc prior: 57.4% vs 45%).
3. **Supervisor headroom**: top-spread-quintile mean h2h_market. < −4 pts → build Phase B; flat → kill the System-2 seam permanently and bank the simplicity.
4. **Cap economics**: how often caps bind, and capped-and-wrong count — determines whether asymmetric caps or width changes are even live questions.
5. **Parse failure rate**: >2–3% of member-runs → build reprompt repair; <1% → delete `parser_model`.
6. **Resolved-state catastrophes**: any ≥0.95-wrong events → the criteria-gate jumps the queue (single worst failure mode in winner reports).
7. **Market signal**: price_at_T coverage %, corr(member_mean, price) (parroting), and whether `market_echo` beats the bot on crosswalked questions → sets blend weight w and whether prices leave the prompts.

10. **Date-lag damage**: residuals on resolution−close >14d questions → sizes the close-date bug fix priority.
